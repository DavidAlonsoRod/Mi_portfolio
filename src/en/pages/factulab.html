Description

Implementation of VeriFactu inside Factulab, the invoicing and management software by VEGA CLOUD SOLUTIONS.

Each invoice generates a compliant record (RD 1007/2023 / Order HAC/1177/2024): submission to AEAT, chained fingerprint, tax QR in the UI and responsible declaration of the invoicing IT system (SIF).

Includes remittance statuses, send-error handling and VeriFactu block preview in the billing flow.
