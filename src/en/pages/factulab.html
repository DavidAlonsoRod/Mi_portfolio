Description

B2B multi-tenant SaaS covering production, billing, accounting and a branded online store for photo labs. These are Factulab’s main features:

  • Intake of customer-supplied images for printing on different media
  • Invoicing of orders coming from the online store
  • Accounting for the company’s operations
  • AI-assisted scanning of purchase invoices for import into accounting
  • Branded online store for selling products customized with images and text
  • Trello-style task management assigned to employees
  • Customer portal to view orders and invoices
  • Spanish e-invoicing with VeriFactu (AEAT)
  • Integrations: Redsys payments, PGC accounting, IMAP purchase invoices and WooCommerce

Multi-tenant Flask API (SQLAlchemy, Alembic, JWT, PostgreSQL) with per-company isolation, Gunicorn in production and domain-based branding.

Browser online designer (Canvas 2D, SVG, vector PDF with PyMuPDF/FontTools) for print-ready layouts, plus a buyer-facing customization flow.
