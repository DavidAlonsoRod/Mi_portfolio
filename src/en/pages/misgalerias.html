Description

SaaS for photographers: client galleries (/g/:slug) with password or link access, photo selection, comments and original downloads. Studio back-office for clients, contracts, quotes, invoices and session booking.

Browser uploads go straight to S3 (presigned PUT); Flask only signs URLs and stores metadata. Gallery auth is isolated from the print-lab app (JWT cookies, CORS).
